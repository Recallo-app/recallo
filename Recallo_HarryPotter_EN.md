# Nachschlag
## The Houses of Hogwarts
Overview
- **Gryffindor** – Courage, incl. Harry, Ron, Hermione
- **Slytherin** – Ambition, incl. Draco Malfoy, Snape
- **Hufflepuff** – Loyalty
- **Ravenclaw** – Wisdom

## Important Items
Magical artifacts
- **Wand** – each wizard's individual tool
- **Invisibility Cloak** – makes the wearer invisible, a Potter family heirloom
- **Marauder's Map** – shows every person in Hogwarts
