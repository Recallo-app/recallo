# Nachschlag
## The Fellowship of the Ring
Members
- 4 Hobbits: Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin
- 2 Men: Aragorn, Boromir
- Gandalf (wizard), Legolas (elf), Gimli (dwarf)

## The One Ring
Origin and purpose
- Forged by Sauron in Mount Doom
- Grants power, corrupts its bearer
- Can only be destroyed in the fire of Mount Doom
