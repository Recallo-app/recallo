# Nachschlag
## The Main Crew of the Enterprise-D
Quick overview
- **Jean-Luc Picard** – Captain, diplomatic and composed
- **William Riker** – First Officer
- **Data** – Android, strives for humanity
- **Worf** – Klingon Chief of Security
- **Deanna Troi** – Counselor, empathic Betazoid
- **Geordi La Forge** – Chief Engineer, blind, uses a VISOR

## The Borg
Central antagonists
- Collective consciousness, no individualism
- Famous line: "Resistance is futile"
- Assimilate the technology and biology of other species
