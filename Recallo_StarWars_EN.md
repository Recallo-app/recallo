# Nachschlag
## The Three Films of the Original Trilogy
Episodes IV, V, and VI at a glance
- **Episode IV – A New Hope** (1977): Luke joins the Rebellion, the first Death Star is destroyed
- **Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back** (1980): Escape from Hoth, training with Yoda, Vader's revelation
- **Episode VI – Return of the Jedi** (1983): Rescuing Han Solo, the Battle of Endor, the Emperor's fall

## Key Factions
Rebel Alliance vs. Galactic Empire
- **Rebel Alliance**: fights against the Empire's tyranny, including Leia, Han, Luke
- **Galactic Empire**: led by Emperor Palpatine and Darth Vader

## The Force
Light and dark side
- **Jedi**: use the light side of the Force, guardians of peace
- **Sith**: use the dark side, driven by power and anger
- Known Jedi: Obi-Wan Kenobi, Yoda, Luke Skywalker
- Known Sith: Darth Vader, Emperor Palpatine
